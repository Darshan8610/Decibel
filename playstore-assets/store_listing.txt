Experience music like never before with Decibel – your all-in-one modern music player and streaming companion designed with sleek Material You aesthetics and powerful audio performance.

Whether streaming your favorite tracks online or enjoying your offline library, Decibel delivers crystal-clear sound, real-time synced lyrics, and seamless playback across all your devices.

🎵 KEY FEATURES:

• Online Streaming & Search:
  Discover millions of songs, albums, and playlists with instant high-quality streaming and intelligent recommendations.

• Synced Word-by-Word Lyrics:
  Sing along effortlessly with real-time synchronized lyrics for thousands of songs.

• Advanced Audio Engine & Equalizer:
  Fine-tune your acoustic experience with a multi-band equalizer, bass booster, 3D audio virtualizer, and volume preamp.

• Android Auto Support:
  Take your music on the road safely with official Android Auto media templates and intuitive in-car controls.

• Offline Caching & Local Music:
  Listen anywhere, anytime. Seamlessly manage and play local MP3, FLAC, AAC, and WAV files with automatic metadata and album art extraction.

• Material You Theming:
  Enjoy dynamic theming that adapts to your album artwork or system wallpaper with pure Dark Mode support.

• Smart Sleep Timer & Queue Management:
  Fall asleep to your favorite soothing tunes with a customizable sleep timer, drag-and-drop queue management, and playlist curation.

• Privacy-First & Lightweight:
  No invasive tracking, no ads during playback, and designed with minimal battery and memory overhead.

Elevate your audio journey today with Decibel!
